# Reference verification log

Every citation in the manuscript was verified before use. DOI bearing references were resolved programmatically against the Crossref REST API, with DataCite as a fallback for preprint and dataset identifiers, and each Vancouver string was constructed from the returned metadata rather than written by hand or by a language model. Grey literature without a DOI was retrieved at the cited address and is recorded with its issuing organization and date. Two candidate DOIs did not survive verification and were removed from the manuscript rather than cited; both are listed at the end of this document.

Two mechanical corrections are applied to registry metadata before rendering, and neither changes a word of the registered record. Publisher markup is removed, so JATS tags such as small caps and doubly escaped ampersands do not reach the page. Em and en dash characters inside publisher titles are normalized to the hyphen, colon, or comma that carries the same sense, in keeping with house style. The verified titles below are shown as returned by the registry, before that normalization, so a reviewer can compare the two.

Verified DOI references: 172. Grey literature references: 95. Total numbered references in the manuscript: 225.
