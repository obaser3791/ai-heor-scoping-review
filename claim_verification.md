## What is not verifiable here

Three limitations bear on the claims above and are stated in the manuscript rather than buried in this log.

First, the blinded reference standard is a large language model classifier rather than dual human review. It is a reference standard, not truth. The correct next step is to substitute human dual review on the same 300 record sample and recompute every statistic with `bibliometrics/04_agreement.py`.

Second, PubMed is a living index. Re-running the search after 9 September 2026 will return a different record set. The retrieved records are shipped in `data/` so that the analysis is reproducible independently of the search.

Third, verified reference titles are reproduced exactly as returned by Crossref, including any punctuation the publisher used in the original title. Source fidelity takes precedence over house style inside a citation.
